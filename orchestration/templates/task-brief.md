## Standards

- **A claim needs evidence.** A number, a citation, or a reproduction. Not a recollection.
- **A check that cannot fail is a defect.** For every test you add or touch, say what change would break it.
- **Never adjust an expectation to make a change pass.** If your change moves a pinned value, your change is
  wrong until shown otherwise. If you believe the expectation was wrong, say so with the evidence.
- **A fixture is not evidence.** A test authored to match the implementation says nothing about which side is
  wrong when the two disagree. Settle it from the primary source.
- **A disagreement is the valuable output.** Where two sources conflict, say which is right and on what evidence.
  Do not average them and do not keep both.
- **Name what you could not determine.** An honest unknown is a result; a guess presented as a finding is a
  defect the next unit will inherit.
- **Report a refuted prediction prominently.** If this brief's stated expectation is contradicted by
  measurement, that is a good outcome and should be written as one.
